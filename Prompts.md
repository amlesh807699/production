
## Prompt 1 for ui/ux from stich
Design a premium, modern, production-quality responsive landing page for a digital marketing and technology company called **“Prodesk IT”**.

The website should feel like a professional real-world IT/digital agency website, not a basic student assignment.

### Design Direction

* Modern SaaS / digital agency aesthetic
* Clean, minimal and premium
* Dark-first visual design with a sophisticated light-mode alternative
* Use a deep navy/black background with subtle blue-purple gradients
* High-quality typography with strong visual hierarchy
* Generous whitespace
* Subtle glassmorphism and soft shadows
* Rounded cards with professional spacing
* Avoid excessive gradients, excessive animations, or clutter
* The overall design should look suitable for a professional company portfolio

### Page Structure

#### 1. Sticky Navbar

Create a sticky navigation bar at the top.

Left:

* Prodesk IT logo/wordmark

Right:

* Home
* About
* Services
* Contact
* Dark/Light mode toggle
* Primary “Get Started” button

On mobile:

* Show logo
* Hamburger menu
* Navigation links should open inside a clean mobile menu

Use a subtle glassmorphism effect on the navbar:

* Semi-transparent background
* Backdrop blur
* Thin border
* High readability

#### 2. Hero Section

Create a visually impressive hero section.

Small eyebrow text:
“DIGITAL SOLUTIONS FOR MODERN BUSINESS”

Main headline:
“We Build Digital Experiences That Move Your Business Forward.”

Supporting text:
“We design and develop fast, scalable and user-focused digital experiences that help businesses grow.”

Primary CTA:
“Get Started →”

Secondary CTA:
“Explore Services”

Add a modern abstract technology visual on the right side using:

* Gradient orbs
* Abstract UI cards
* Floating interface elements
* Subtle grid/background patterns

Do not make the hero overly complicated.

#### 3. Trust / Stats Section

Add a small credibility section below the hero.

Example statistics:

* 50+ Projects
* 100% Responsive
* 24/7 Support

Use clean typography and subtle separators.

#### 4. About Section

Create an “About Prodesk IT” section.

Heading:
“Technology Meets Creativity”

Description:
“Prodesk IT combines strategy, technology and creativity to build digital experiences that create measurable impact.”

Include a modern visual/card on the opposite side.

#### 5. Services Section

Heading:
“What We Do”

Supporting text:
“Digital solutions designed around your business goals.”

Create exactly 3 prominent service cards:

1. Web Development

   * Modern
   * Fast
   * Scalable

2. Digital Marketing

   * Strategy
   * Content
   * Growth

3. SEO Optimization

   * Technical SEO
   * Search visibility
   * Organic growth

Each card should include:

* Minimal icon
* Service number (01, 02, 03)
* Title
* Description
* “Learn More →”

Hover interaction:

* Card slightly moves upward
* Subtle shadow increases
* Border/accent changes
* Smooth transition

#### 6. CTA Section

Create a visually strong call-to-action section.

Heading:
“Ready to Build Something Great?”

Description:
“Let's turn your idea into a powerful digital experience.”

Button:
“Get Started →”

Use a subtle gradient/abstract background.

#### 7. Footer

Create a professional footer containing:

* Prodesk IT logo/name
* Short company description
* Quick Links
* Services
* Social media icons
* Copyright

Example:
“© 2026 Prodesk IT. All rights reserved.”

### Responsive Requirements

The design must work perfectly on:

* Desktop: 1440px
* Laptop: 1024px
* Tablet: 768px
* Mobile: 390px
* Mobile: 320px

Desktop:

* Two-column hero
* Three-column services

Tablet:

* Adapt spacing and typography
* Services may become 2 columns

Mobile:

* Single-column layout
* Hamburger navigation
* Stacked CTA buttons
* Single-column service cards
* No horizontal scrolling
* Comfortable touch targets

### Accessibility

Design with accessibility in mind:

* Strong color contrast
* Semantic structure
* Clearly visible focus states
* Accessible buttons
* Meaningful icon labels
* Readable typography

### Interaction Design

Include subtle professional micro-interactions:

* Button hover/active states
* Service card lift effect
* Navigation hover states
* Smooth theme transition
* Subtle hero entrance animation

Avoid excessive animations.

### Visual Style

Use:

* Deep navy / black
* White / off-white text
* Blue and purple accent gradients
* Subtle borders
* Soft shadows
* 12–20px border radius
* Modern sans-serif typography

The final result should look like a **real professional digital agency website that could be deployed publicly**, while remaining clean, fast, accessible and easy to implement using HTML, raw CSS/Flexbox/Grid and vanilla JavaScript.

## Prompt 2 — Understanding the Problem

### Problem

I encountered a technical problem during the development/deployment of the project and wanted to understand the root cause instead of blindly applying a fix.

### AI Prompt

> How can I solve this problem? Give me a deep explanation of what is causing the problem, why it is happening, and how I can fix it step by step.

### Purpose

The purpose of this prompt was to understand the root cause of the issue and learn the correct development/debugging process rather than simply copying a solution.

### Outcome

The explanation helped me understand the cause of the issue, the required changes, and how to verify that the problem was fixed.
